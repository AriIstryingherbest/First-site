    Stay Curious and Keep on Thinking

    Welcome, have you ever wondered how doctors can help solve a mystery after someone dies? Forensic pathologists use science, medicine, and careful investigation to discover what happened. They examine evidence and use their medical knowledge to determine the cause and manner of death. Their work can help families understand what happened and can also provide important information for criminal investigations. Becoming a forensic pathologist takes years of education and training, but it is a career that combines medicine, science, and solving mysteries.It is also a job that has a good paying amount depending on where you live.
